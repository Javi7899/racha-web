In one sentence: Racha collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and sends nothing to any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.

What is stored, and where

Your habits, what you log, your notes and your settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. With iCloud on, the app keeps a copy in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account, so you can restore it on a new phone. I have no access to that copy.

Health

With your permission, Racha reads from Health only the data types your habits use (for example steps, sleep, workouts or water) to tick them automatically. It never writes to Health, never sends that data off your device, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with anyone.

Location

If you create a habit tied to a place, like the gym, iOS tells Racha when you arrive in the area you picked. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.

Screen Time

App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time system. Apple hides which apps you choose, even from Racha: the app only knows that a rule exists and whether it is met.

Weekly review

The weekly review is written by Apple Intelligence on your own iPhone. Nothing is sent to any server.

Purchases

Racha Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. I receive no data about you or your payment.

What there isn't

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No third-party components that collect data.

Crash reports

If you turned on "Share With App Developers", Apple may send me anonymous technical crash reports. Apple handles them, and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Since the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.
