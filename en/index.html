Racha: habits on autopilot

Support Privacy Español

Racha is a habit tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch that barely needs a tap: habits tick themselves from what your iPhone already measures (steps, sleep, workouts, water, arriving at the gym), and the rest is one tap from a widget, the Lock Screen, your Watch or Siri. It's free with five habits, and Racha Pro is a single payment. Never a subscription.

Questions

A habit didn't tick itself

Racha can only read what Health allows. Open Health, tap your picture, then Privacy › Apps › Racha, and turn on what your habits use. Apple Watch readings can take a few minutes to reach your iPhone.

I forgot to log yesterday

On Today, tap the day in the week at the top and tick it. You can also tap any day in a habit's calendar.

What happens if I miss a day?

One miss is forgiven: the streak waits and the flame turns grey. A second miss in a row ends it. Going on holiday? Use Settings › Pause All Habits.

An app is still blocked after I did the habit

Earn It rules lift when Racha sees the habit done. If you logged it from a widget or your Watch, open Racha once and the apps unlock.

New iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and install Racha: the first screen offers to restore your habits from iCloud. To get Racha Pro back, go to Settings › Restore Purchase. The purchase can be shared with Family Sharing.

Where are my habits stored?

On your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud Drive. There are no accounts and no servers.
